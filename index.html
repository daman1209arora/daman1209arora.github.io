I am a third year PhD student at the Machine Learning Department at Carnegie Mellon University. I am interested in the following questions:

  • how do we make AI systems better at reasoning
  • how can we enable continual learning in the era of modern Deep Learning

I am fortunate to be advised by Andrea Zanette. Previously, I worked as a Research Fellow at Microsoft Research India, where I worked on Retrieval and Software Agents with Nagarajan Natarajan. Before that, I spent my undergrad at IIT Delhi where I worked on Generalized Planning with Mausam and Parag Singla.
